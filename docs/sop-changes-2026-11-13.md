# Call Confirmation Quick Guide: changes since the Nov 3 version

*Compiled Nov 13, 2026 from #cc-gino (Nov 9–13) and the tracker build. Base document: "Call Confirmation Quick Guide", Nov 3, 2026. The updated guide is [`SOP.md`](SOP.md); section 2 below records how each open decision was settled.*

Already in the Nov 3 guide, so not repeated here: no-computer steps, can't-find-the-invite steps, and booking every recovery with an Enrollment Specialist.

## 1. New rules to add

### Setup
- **Close phone number.** If you don't have one, create one in Close. Any US area code is fine. *(Sheila, Nov 10)*

### Who to call
- **The closer already confirmed the call.** Don't call. Leave the prospect to the closer and log **Closer Confirmed**. If they book again later and the closer hasn't reached them, treat them as a fresh booking. *(Sheila, Nov 12)*
- **A setter tried before but never reached them.** Nothing changes: confirm as normal. *(Jay, Nov 11)*
- **Automated texts went out before your call.** That's by design. Still call anyone who hasn't accepted. *(Sheila, Nov 12)*

### Checking whether someone accepted
If the lead in Close shows no calendar details:
1. Look for the "Accepted" email on the lead in Close.
2. Check for the green check mark next to the guest on the calendar invite.
3. Check whether the person has a second lead profile.

*(Sheila and Jay, Nov 12)*

### Invite problems
- **Typo in the prospect's email address.** Ask the closer to fix the Google Calendar invite. *(Jay, Nov 11)*

### 5:45pm MT (7:45pm ET) day-before cancel
- **Never** cancel a booking made that same day.
- **Cancel** next-day calls that still haven't accepted after your calls, voicemails and texts.

*(Sheila, Nov 12; Jay agreed)*

### Logging in Close
New options in the **Call Confirmation** field:

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| `8. Unreachable - Cancelled before CC contact` | They cancelled before you reached them. These stay in the list for recovery later. |
| `9. Unreachable - Confirmed before CC contact` | They accepted before you reached them, including someone you couldn't reach on the day-before call who accepted afterwards. |
| `Closer Confirmed` | The closer already confirmed them (see "Who to call"). |
| `Setter Confirmed` | A setter already confirmed them. |

- **Lead status.** Change it from **Needs Confirmation** to **Confirmed** by hand every time you confirm someone. There's no automation for it. *(Jay, Nov 13)*
- **Smart view.** "Call Confirmation – Meeting Tomorrow" now hides options 8 and 9, Setter Confirmed and Closer Confirmed. *(Sheila, Nov 12)*

## 4. Decided for the tracker

- **Accepted when they booked.** No welcome call is needed (now in the SOP).
- **Fridays.** The day-before call and 5:45 cancel on Friday cover **Saturday calls only**. Sunday and Monday calls have no day-before call or 5:45 cancel. *(Sheila, Nov 13)*
- **Weekly KPIs and bonus: kept out of the SOP until they're final.** The tracker uses them for now:
  - KPIs are checked every week (Monday to Sunday), starting the week of Nov 23.
  - 100% confirmation, after excused misses.
  - At least 85% of during-shift welcome calls dialed within 15 minutes.
  - Zero logging errors that count as misses.
  - Excused misses: a step he couldn't do (no voicemail option, a full mailbox, a text that won't send) isn't a miss.
  - Bonus = recovered calls that showed × $50.
